**The applicable principles are trusted launch ownership, an independent parent-loss channel, and separate root/descendant exit proofs. Linux native subreaping covers new groups/sessions. The group route cannot replace AgentLoom's established inherited-token detached Shell contract.**

EOF alone cannot certify descendants gone. [owned-stdio:25–35, 51–89](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/59cb44796b0f1f0daa0e37af8b1495abb45d9bc1/src/process/owned-stdio.ts#L51) |

Group Worker cleanup retains the observer until root and lineage settle, including nested relays; it signals in-group members, so arbitrary escaped-child termination cannot be inferred. ACK releases the sender and does not replace the independent extinction join. Full Worker lifecycle persists physical identity, checks durable state/abort/current owner before opening the start gate and again before business descriptor dispatch, and retains an owner for deferred cleanup. Recovery lacking extinction proof retains capacity; active ownership is released only after terminal persistence. Bounded observation and full `awaitCleanup` are distinct. These are the complete Worker protocol and do not make the generic argv owner a substitute for DBOS. [child-lifecycle:253–321, 493–505, 609–620](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/59cb44796b0f1f0daa0e37af8b1495abb45d9bc1/src/node-host/node-worker-child-lifecycle.ts#L253), [recovery:32–99, 166–182, 231–241, 293–345](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/blob/59cb44796b0f1f0daa0e37af8b1495abb45d9bc1/src/node-host/node-worker-supervisor-recovery.ts#L32).

**Implication for the current fix:** use host-created scoped generation, propagate through Pi/tool/MCP minimal or explicit environments, prevent external environment from taking sibling custody, and retain process-instance identity for signal targets. On macOS/Linux the same public entry must prove marked detached, root-first and top-Python SIGKILL cleanup before recovery/protection cleanup. AgentLoom already promises inherited process-token cleanup in [Pi README:87–89](../../src/runtimes/pi/README.md), with the wider ownership and pre-recovery matrix in [Spec:113–114, 142](../specs/default-managed-worker-execution-v3.md). Upstream group limitations cannot narrow those requirements.
